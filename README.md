# Listy

Reusable lists for your shop, your workouts and everything else, on Windows and Android. Your lists are saved in your own Google Drive and stay in sync between your devices.

## Download

Downloads will appear on the [Releases page](../../releases/latest). There are none yet.

### Windows

1. Download `Listy-<version>-setup.exe`.
2. Open it. Windows may warn you because the app isn't signed yet. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Android

1. Download `Listy-<version>.apk` on your phone.
2. Open it, and allow your browser to install apps if asked.

## Your data

There's no Listy server. Your lists are stored in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only Listy can use. You can delete them at any time from **Account** in the app.

The app's source code isn't in this repository, which only holds the downloads.
